Decision: Include identifiers in some filenames, such as 0001-first-slice.md and UC-01.md. 

Alternative: Use descriptive filenames without identifiers. If chosen, filenames would describe each document without those labels.

Why we rejected it: Descriptive names are easier to read, but without identifiers it may be harder to match files to requirements or refer to a specific document as the project grows.

Cost of our decision: The identifiers make filenames longer. We also have to keep them consistent when adding or renaming documents which might lead to communication conflicts.
