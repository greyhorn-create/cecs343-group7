## Decision 0003:
If the matching rules grow, move the filtering into a pure function in a new tutor_matching.py; update app.py to call it and adapt the subject-search test. 

Alternative considered: Keep the filtering logic in find_tutors() in app.py and increasing our organization to avoid making more modules.

Why we rejected it: Keeping everything in one file avoids adding a module and changing imports. But as more matching rules are added, find_tutors() could become harder to understand because it already handles the Flask request, validates the subject, filters tutors, and builds the JSON response.

Cost of our decision: We’ll need to add and maintain tutor_matching.py, update app.py and the test, and keep their interfaces consistent. That means changes may involve more than one file.
