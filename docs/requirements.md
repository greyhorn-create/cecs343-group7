Purpose: Tutors are able to set-up their own sessions, with details like subject, time, multi-person/one-on-one, and price range. Students will then be able to schedule tutors depending on the requirements that they input, and will  be able to cancel if they wish. 

Actors: This system will be touched and interacted with by students with academic needs, qualified tutors in search for a job, and moderators to ensure everything is running smoothly 

Functional: #1 The program will be able to sort through a list of tutors based off the requirements need by the students 
            #2 The ability for Parents of students (17 or younger) to have profiles checking in on their Son/Daughter.
            #3 The program will be able to record information for the users (studetns/tutors/Parents).
            #4 Tutors will be able to put a price on their session if they want to.
            #5 Students will be able to rate tutors and leave review if they wish to.

Quality:
At least 95% of tutor searches shall return results within 2 seconds
Zero unauthorized disclosures of private student schedules or account information shall occur
No confirmed profile, price, schedule, or review data shall be lost, with service restored within 4 hours

Out of scope: In this program there will profiles for parents of students who are minors(ages below 18) that allows them to see the students activity and notify the parent that way parents can monitor students if they wish so. There will also be protection of privacy for the student/tutor/parent profiles which will keep there personal information private to other user, that way every user will feel assure that the website would be save to use and that no one will be able to obtain sensitive information.
